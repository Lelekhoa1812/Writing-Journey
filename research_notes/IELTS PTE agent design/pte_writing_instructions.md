## What are the current official constraints for Summarize Written Text and Write Essay?

### Takeaway

For a test taken now, Summarize Written Text is two items. Each gives 10 minutes to turn a passage of up to 300 words into one sentence of 5–75 words. Write Essay is one item. It gives 20 minutes for an argumentative essay. Full form credit is 200–300 words. Those word bands were already the rule in 2019. They did not change in 2025.

### Cited Findings

- The score guide says its contents, with the Pearson site, are the only official information about PTE Academic. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- A test has 65–75 questions and 22 question types. Part 1, Speaking and Writing, is about 76–84 minutes. Summarize Written Text is listed as 2 questions. Write Essay is listed as 1 question. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The July 2025 research report’s design table moves Summarize Written Text from 1 to 2 and keeps Write Essay at 1. The same report says the updated test is about 65 scored questions, 22 question types, still about 2 hours, in the same three parts. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf)
- Updates apply to any PTE Academic or PTE Academic UKVI test taken after 7 August 2025. PTE Core and PTE Home were not changed. Two new question types were added, both in Speaking and Writing, and both are spoken: Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation. Pearson says the original 20 question types remain, and that other scoring changes do not change how candidates should prepare. — [PTE Academic updates 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-updates-2025/)
- Summarize Written Text: read a text of up to 300 words, write one sentence, 10 minutes. The response must be one sentence, capital at the start and one full stop at the end, and between 5 and 75 words. Fewer than five words or more than 75 words is not scored. The on-screen Total Word Count is the counter the candidate is told to use. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/); same 5–75 and “will not be scored” rule in [Writing test tips, V4 July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/pte-academic-writing-test-tips.pdf)
- The score guide’s form rule matches that word range and adds the one-sentence rule: form credit requires one single complete sentence. Form is 0 if it is not one sentence, if it has fewer than 5 or more than 75 words, or if it is written in capital letters. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Write Essay: one argumentative essay, 20 minutes. The format page tells the candidate to write at least 200 words and no more than 300. Full form credit is 200–300 words. Length outside 200–300 lowers the score. Under 120 or over 380 receives no score points on any of the seven factors. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Score-guide form bands, current: 2 points for 200–300 words; 1 point for 120–199 or 301–380 words; 0 if under 120 or over 380, or if the essay is in capital letters, has no punctuation, or is only bullet points or very short sentences. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The same three form bands, including 200–300 as full credit, are in the June 2023 guide and in the October 2019 guide. The optimal word range did not move. — [Score Guide Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf); [Score Guide Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download)
- July 2025 tips still say: do not write fewer than 200 or more than 300 words; do write 200–300; plan, then leave 2–3 minutes to check. They also say an off-topic essay, or one with a significant amount of pre-prepared scripted material, gets no score for the question. — [Writing test tips, V4 July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/pte-academic-writing-test-tips.pdf)
- Official prompt guidance is not a closed catalogue. The format page says the essay is argumentative, that prompts “can be written in a number of ways,” and gives two examples: discuss whether you agree or disagree with a statement, and consider how different groups of people might feel about a situation. The candidate is told to find the topic, the points that must be answered, and any points of view to present. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Older item counts, for comparison only. October 2019: Summarize Written Text 2–3 items, Write Essay 1–2 items, Part 1 about 77–93 minutes. June 2023: Summarize Written Text 1–2, Write Essay 1–2, Part 1 about 54–67 minutes, and the whole test 52–64 tasks across 20 task types. — [Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download); [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- A Pearson preparation article still says the candidate will do 2–3 Summarize Written Text questions, that a good sentence is 20–35 words, and that an essay under 200 or over 380 scores zero. That article conflicts with the 2026 score guide and the current format page. Treat it as outdated. — [Part 1 writing self-improvement](https://www.pearsonpte.com/articles/part-1-writing-self-improvement/); contradicted by [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf) and [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)

### Inferences

- The live constraint set is the 2026 score guide plus the format page, not coaching sites and not the self-improvement article.
- “200–300 words” is the full-credit band. It is not the zero boundary. The zero boundary is under 120 or over 380, plus the non-prose conditions.
- Pearson has not published a required essay structure such as “four paragraphs.” The format page says the best responses have multiple paragraphs and a clear introduction, body, and conclusion. That is a quality description, not a form rule.
- Agree/disagree and different-groups prompts are official examples. Problem-solution, advantages-disadvantages, and similar labels are coaching categories unless they appear in the prompt itself.

### Gaps

- No official definition was found of how the on-screen word counter treats hyphens, numbers, or contractions.
- No official maximum sentence count for the essay was found. “Very short sentences” is a form-zero condition, not a number.
- No closed official list of essay prompt types was found beyond “argumentative” plus the two examples on the format page.
- The July 2025 research report’s “current” column says 1 Summarize Written Text item. The June 2023 score guide says 1–2. Both are Pearson documents. They do not state the date on which the live count became exactly 1 before the August 2025 move to exactly 2.

## Which traits are scored, and what are the raw point ranges?

### Takeaway

Summarize Written Text has four traits and a summed maximum of 9 raw points: Content 0–4, Form 0–1, Grammar 0–2, Vocabulary 0–2. Write Essay has seven traits and a summed maximum of 26: Content 0–6, Form 0–2, Development, Structure and Coherence 0–6, Grammar 0–2, General Linguistic Range 0–6, Vocabulary Range 0–2, Spelling 0–2. Pearson does not publish those sums as a reported score. Before 7 August 2025 the short scales were Content 0–2 on the summary and Content, Development/Structure/Coherence, and General Linguistic Range each 0–3 or 0–2 on the essay.

### Cited Findings

- Summarize Written Text is partial credit. It contributes to Reading and to Writing. Current traits and top points: Content 4, Form 1, Grammar 2, Vocabulary 2. There is no spelling trait and no separate coherence trait on this item. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of the current summary Content scale: 4 means the passage is covered, main ideas are synthesised, extra detail is left out, and connectives vary; 3 means adequate coverage with some missing ideas or uneven paraphrase; 2 means partial coverage, with main points and side detail not separated, and heavy copying; 1 means relevant but not a real summary, with main ideas missing or distorted; 0 means too limited to score higher and no comprehension of the source. Flow and connective use sit inside these Content levels. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of summary Grammar: 2 correct structure; 1 errors that do not block communication; 0 structure that could block communication. Vocabulary: 2 appropriate words; 1 lexical errors that do not block communication; 0 word choice that could block communication. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The format page still describes four factors for the summary: Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary. Best grammar responses usually have a main clause and a subordinate clause. Vocabulary credit includes relevance, academic appropriateness, and effective synonyms. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- July 2025 research: most rubrics were not changed. Summarize Written Text Content was widened to 0–4. For Write Essay, Content, Development Structure and Coherence, and General Linguistic Range were widened to 0–6. The 0–6 width was chosen so raters could separate levels reliably. A 6 is not a CEFR C2. The scale is meant to span A1 to C1/C2. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf)
- Write Essay is partial credit and contributes to Writing only. The format page says the score uses seven factors. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Essay trait maxima in the 2026 score guide: Content 6; Form 2; Development, Structure and Coherence 6; Grammar 2; General Linguistic Range 6; Vocabulary Range 2; Spelling 2. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of essay Content: 6 is a full, specific argument in the candidate’s own words, with support throughout; 5 is persuasive with minor gaps; 4 addresses the main point but lacks depth, and support is uneven; 3 is on topic but misses the main points, with weak support; 2 is superficial, generic, or stuck to the prompt wording; 1 shows incomplete understanding and disjointed support; 0 does not deal with the prompt. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of Development, Structure and Coherence: 6 is easy to follow, with a developed introduction and conclusion, logical paragraphs, and varied connectives; 5 is conventional and mostly smooth; 4 is mostly conventional but some elements are missing or poorly linked; 3 has only traces of that shape; 2 has little recognisable structure; 1 is disconnected ideas; 0 has no recognisable structure. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of essay Grammar: 2 is consistent control of complex language, with rare errors; 1 is relatively high control and no mistakes that cause misunderstanding; 0 is mainly simple structures and/or several basic mistakes. The format page titles this factor “Grammar usage and mechanics” and says punctuation and capitalisation are examined. The score guide does not add an eighth trait for mechanics. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Paraphrase of General Linguistic Range: 6 is varied, precise language with no real limit on what can be said, and any errors are rare and minor; lower bands narrow the range, force repetition, and then block meaning; 0 means meaning is not accessible. This is a separate trait from Vocabulary Range. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Paraphrase of Vocabulary Range: 2 is a broad repertoire, including idioms and colloquial language where they fit; 1 is a good academic range with some imprecision; 0 is mainly basic vocabulary, not enough for the topic. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Spelling: 2 if correct; 1 if there is one spelling error; 0 if there is more than one. PTE Academic accepts US, UK, Australian, and Canadian spelling, but one convention should be used consistently in a response. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- June 2023, and the same widths in October 2019, for Summarize Written Text: Content 0–2, Form 0–1, Grammar 0–2, Vocabulary 0–2. Content 2 was a good summary with the relevant aspects mentioned; 1 missed one or two aspects; 0 omitted or misrepresented the main aspects. — [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf); [Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download)
- June 2023 Write Essay maxima: Content 0–3, Form 0–2, Development, structure and coherence 0–2, Grammar 0–2, General linguistic range 0–2, Vocabulary range 0–2, Spelling 0–2. Content 3 “adequately deals with the prompt”; 0 includes not dealing with the prompt and includes a significant amount of pre-prepared or memorised material. — [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- Who scores what, from 7 August 2025. All question types are AI-scored. Summarize Written Text and Write Essay also get a human review of Content before the task score is final. Write Essay also gets a human review of Development, Structure and Coherence, and of General Linguistic Range. The format page says the same seven essay traits are AI-scored, with that human review on Content, development/structure/coherence, and linguistic range. The research report says the human and machine trait scores are combined, and that grammar, vocabulary range, pronunciation, and fluency stay with the automated system. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/); [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf)
- The score guide’s worked scoring diagram is for Describe Image, not for writing. In that diagram, if AI and the human disagree on Content, a second human makes the final Content judgement. Pronunciation and fluency stay AI-only. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)

### Inferences

- Adding the published maxima gives 9 raw points for one summary and 26 for one essay. Those totals are a sum a coaching app can show. They are not a number Pearson prints on the score report.
- “Coherence” is not an item trait. On the summary it is inside Content. On the essay it is one part of Development, Structure and Coherence, which also covers paragraphing, argument development, and connectives.
- General Linguistic Range is not Vocabulary Range. An app that has one “vocabulary” score is collapsing two essay traits, one of which is now three times as wide as the other.
- The second-human rule is published for Describe Image. It is not safe to assume the same adjudication sentence applies to the essay unless Pearson says so. What is safe: Content, and for the essay also development/structure/coherence and linguistic range, are hybrid.

### Gaps

- No official rule was found for how a spelling mistake on Summarize Written Text is treated, because that item has no spelling trait.
- No official rule was found for whether mixed US/UK spelling counts as one spelling error, two errors, or a consistency penalty beyond the format page’s “one convention” instruction.
- No public table was found that converts the new 0–6 descriptions into the old 0–2 or 0–3 descriptions. They are new scales, not a relabeling of the old points.
- The exact numeric blend of human and AI, when they disagree on an essay trait, was not published in the sources reviewed.

## How does Pearson report the 10–90 score, and how should an app map traits onto it?

### Takeaway

Pearson reports an overall score and four communicative scores, each from 10 to 90. The overall score is not the average of the four skill scores. Trait points are not reported. An app that scores Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary, and Coherence from 10 to 90 and averages them is not estimating the reported Writing score. That average uses the wrong traits, the wrong weights, and the wrong scale.

### Cited Findings

- The score report is an overall score plus Listening, Reading, Speaking, and Writing. Each ranges from 10 to 90. The overall score is performance across the whole test. The guide states that the overall score is not an average of the communicative scores. The July 2025 institution guide says the same. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Institution Score Guide, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/Score-Guide-Institution-PTE-Academic-July-2025-web.pdf)
- Integrated items feed every communicative skill they assess. The 2026 guide’s Writing column includes Summarize Written Text (Reading and Writing), Write Essay (Writing), Summarize Spoken Text (Listening and Writing), and Write from Dictation (Listening and Writing, one point per correct word). — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- From the August 2025 redesign, some formerly integrated items were moved to a single skill. The research report names Answer Short Question, Fill in the Blanks, and Read Aloud. In the 2026 guide, both Fill in the Blanks types are Reading only. In the June 2023 guide, Reading and writing: Fill in the blanks still contributed to Reading and Writing. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- Item flow, as published: score Content and Form; if either is 0, the response gets no score points; if Content is above 0, other traits are scored; the question total contributes to the relevant communicative score and to the overall score. The worked example is Describe Image, but the zero rule is stated for responses in general and the examples include an off-topic essay and an essay under 120 words. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Before November 2021 the report also showed enabling skills: grammar, oral fluency, pronunciation, spelling, vocabulary, and written discourse. Pearson removed them from the score report and replaced them with a Skills Profile. The profile is for the test taker, is not shared with institutions, and shows eight categories as performance bars, including Open Response Speaking and Writing, Extended Writing, and Short Writing. It is not a set of 10–90 trait scores. The June 2023 guide says enabling skills were never used for visas or by institutions, and that the change applies to score reports for tests taken before 16 November 2021 versus after. — [Score report and Skills Profile, 16 December 2021](https://www.pearsonpte.com/articles/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-pte-score-report-and-skills-report/); [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- The December 2021 Pearson article says the 10–90 overall score grades English against the Global Scale of English. The 2026 score guide’s reported-scores section describes the 10–90 range and does not, in the pages reviewed, restate the name “Global Scale of English.” It does give CEFR alignment, unchanged from the previous guide, and an IELTS alignment updated from research released in July 2025. — [Score report and Skills Profile, 16 December 2021](https://www.pearsonpte.com/articles/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-pte-score-report-and-skills-report/); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- October 2019: Content and Form contribute to overall and communicative scores. They do not contribute to enabling-skill scores. Grammar, vocabulary, and spelling on the essay feed those enabling skills. Development, structure and coherence, and general linguistic range, feed written discourse. — [Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download)
- Pearson does not publish a formula that turns a trait total into a 10–90 communicative score. Written scoring is described as the Intelligent Essay Assessor and the Knowledge Analysis Technologies engine, trained to match human markers. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)

### Inferences

- A student-facing 10–90 number is defensible only if the app says what it is. A gated raw total, stretched onto 10–90 for one response, can be a coaching display. It is not the Writing score on a score report.
- Equal weights are the wrong model of the raw points. On the essay, Content, Development/Structure/Coherence, and General Linguistic Range each run to 6. Form, Grammar, Vocabulary Range, and Spelling each run to 2. An average of five 10–90 scores gives Form the same vote as Content.
- The app’s five names do not match either item. The summary has no Coherence trait. The essay’s coherence trait is wider than coherence, and the essay also has General Linguistic Range and Spelling, which the five-name average omits.
- Content and Form were never enabling skills on the 10–90 report. Putting them on a 10–90 scale and averaging them with Grammar and Vocabulary recreates a report Pearson did not publish, including before 2021.
- One essay, or one summary, cannot stand in for Writing. Writing also includes the other summary, Summarize Spoken Text, and Write from Dictation. The summary also moves the Reading score. The overall score is a further, non-average combination.
- A usable app sequence is: judge the official raw traits; apply the Content and Form gates before any other credit; show the raw total out of 9 or out of 26; if a 10–90 figure is shown, label it as a display of that one response and state that Pearson does not report it.

### Gaps

- No official conversion from raw trait points, or from an item total, onto 10–90 was found. Any 10–90 figure an app shows is an app scale.
- No official weight of Summarize Written Text versus Write Essay inside the Writing score was found. The essay has more raw points, but Pearson does not say that 26 versus 9 is the contribution ratio after scaling.
- The 2026 score guide does not repeat the November 2021 enabling-skills sentence. Absence of enabling scores in its reported-scores section is consistent with removal. It is not a new 2026 announcement.

## Which form violations zero or cap a trait?

### Takeaway

On both items, Content 0 or Form 0 removes every score point for that response. Other traits can be 0 without wiping the item. Summarize Written Text has no partial form band: any form breach is Form 0 and therefore a wiped item. The essay has a partial band. 120–199 words and 301–380 words cap Form at 1. They do not zero the essay.

### Cited Findings

- General rule: if Content or Form is 0, no score points are given for the response. Published examples of a wiped response: an essay on a completely different topic; an essay of fewer than 120 words; a response that does not deal with the prompt because of a significant amount of pre-prepared or memorised material. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Summarize Written Text, Form 1 only if it is one single complete sentence. Form 0 if it is not one sentence, if it has fewer than 5 or more than 75 words, or if it is written in capital letters. Because Form 0 wipes the response, a second sentence wipes the item even when the word count is inside 5–75. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The format page states the summary zero in candidate language: misreading the topic or purpose scores zero on all four factors; fewer than five words or more than 75 words scores zero on all four factors. The July 2025 tips use the same word-count zero. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/); [Writing test tips, V4 July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/pte-academic-writing-test-tips.pdf)
- Write Essay form: 2 for 200–300 words; 1 for 120–199 or 301–380; 0 for under 120 or over 380, for all capital letters, for no punctuation, or for an answer that is only bullet points or very short sentences. The format page says under 120 or over 380 scores zero on all seven factors, and that writing under 200 or over 300 decreases the score. That decrease is the partial band, not a wipe. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Off-topic essay: the format page says no score points on any of the seven factors. Unoriginal responses get no credit. The candidate must use their own words. — [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/)
- Essay spelling caps only the spelling trait: one error is 1 point, more than one error is 0. The sources reviewed do not say that spelling 0 wipes the other traits. Grammar 0 and vocabulary 0 are likewise trait floors, not item wipes. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The self-improvement article says an essay under 200 or over 380 scores zero, and that more than one summary sentence means “points will be lost.” Both statements conflict with the score guide. Under 200 but at least 120 is Form 1, not a wipe. A second summary sentence is Form 0, and Form 0 wipes the item. — [Part 1 writing self-improvement](https://www.pearsonpte.com/articles/part-1-writing-self-improvement/); contradicted by [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- These form bands are the pre-2025 bands. June 2023 and October 2019 already used 5–75 and one sentence for the summary, and 200–300 / 120–199 or 301–380 / under 120 or over 380 for the essay, including the capital-letter, punctuation, and bullet conditions. — [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf); [Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download)

### Inferences

- The app must gate before it averages. A Form judgement of 10 on a 10–90 scale still leaves the other four traits able to pull the average into a mid or high band. Under Pearson’s rule that response is worth nothing.
- Partial essay length should change Form only: 200–300 maps to the top of Form, 120–199 and 301–380 map to the middle, and only the outside of 120–380 maps to a wiped item.
- All capitals, no punctuation, and bullet-only or very-short-sentence essays are form failures even when the word count is inside 200–300.
- A coaching target such as 20–35 words for the summary is not a scoring band. Inside 5–75, length affects Content only through whether the main ideas fit.

### Gaps

- “Very short sentences” is not defined by a word count or a sentence count.
- No source reviewed said whether a second full stop, a question mark, or a semicolon is enough, by itself, to make a summary “not one sentence,” beyond the candidate instruction to use one full stop.
- No source reviewed said whether Form 1 on a slightly short essay also depresses Content, or only Form.

## What changed in PTE Academic item design through 2025–2026?

### Takeaway

The writing-item change that matters is dated 7 August 2025. Summarize Written Text went to two items and its Content scale widened from 0–2 to 0–4. Write Essay stayed one item, but Content, Development/Structure/Coherence, and General Linguistic Range widened to 0–6, and those traits became hybrid human-and-AI. Word limits, the one-sentence rule, and the 10-minute and 20-minute timings did not change. No later redesign of these two items was found between August 2025 and November 2026.

### Cited Findings

- Effective date: tests taken after 7 August 2025, for PTE Academic and PTE Academic UKVI only. The public update page says the same sections and the original 20 question types remain, two spoken question types were added, some responses are part-scored by humans, humans do not score pronunciation or fluency, the test has slightly more questions and is still about two hours, and some scoring criteria were made more detailed. — [PTE Academic updates 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-updates-2025/)
- Count change in the research report: Summarize Written Text 1 to 2; Write Essay stays 1. The 2026 score guide lists those same counts. Surrounding part length in that guide is about 76–84 minutes, against about 54–67 minutes in the June 2023 guide. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf); [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- Scale change: summary Content 0–4; essay Content, Development Structure and Coherence, and General Linguistic Range 0–6. Grammar, vocabulary, spelling, and essay Form stay on their previous 0–2 widths. The research report says the majority of scoring rubrics were not changed. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf); compared with [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf)
- Scoring process change: hybrid human and automated scoring on extended speaking and writing. Content is hybrid on those tasks. On Write Essay, Development, Structure and Coherence and General Linguistic Range are also hybrid. Automated systems flag possible templated responses so human raters can refuse further scoring. Grammar and vocabulary range stay automated. — [Enhanced PTE Academic, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- What did not change on these two items: one sentence and 5–75 words for the summary; 10 minutes; passage up to 300 words; essay 20 minutes; essay form bands 200–300, 120–199 or 301–380, and under 120 or over 380. Same bands in 2019, 2023, the July 2025 tips, the format page, and the 2026 score guide. — [Version 12, October 2019](https://www.cla.unina.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/EN/D/1%252F0%252F0%252FD.7c2a5df27ab4421ef169/P/BLOB%3AID%3D1580/E/pdf?mode=download); [Speaking and Writing format](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- Related score-report history, older than the item redesign: enabling-skill scores left the score report for tests from 16 November 2021. The 2026 guide still reports only overall and four communicative scores. — [Version 19, June 2023](https://goltc.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PTE_Academic_Score_Guide_for_Test_Takers_June_2023.pdf); [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)
- The 2026 score guide is copyright Pearson Education Ltd 2026 and describes the post-August 2025 test: 65–75 questions, 22 types, human review on the extended writing items, and the widened trait scales. No Pearson page reviewed describes a further writing-item redesign in 2026. — [Score Guide 2026](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf)

### Inferences

- An app built on the pre-August 2025 essay scale (Content out of 3, development and linguistic range out of 2) will mis-rank current scripts. A strong essay can now show differences that the old 0–2 development scale could not.
- An app that still treats “under 200 words” as a zero is applying a rule Pearson’s own older preparation page used, and that the score guide does not use.
- Memorised templates are a current scoring risk, not only a preparation warning. The 2025 design gives human raters a flag for gamed or templated responses.

### Gaps

- No Pearson release note dated 2026 was found that changes Summarize Written Text or Write Essay again after 7 August 2025. “No change found” is not a Pearson statement that the items are frozen.
- The research report’s pre-change count of 1 summary item and the June 2023 range of 1–2 were not reconciled in an official chronology.
- Public scored samples with the post-2025 0–4 and 0–6 marks were not located in this pass. The score guide contains the descriptors. It is not a set of annotated candidate scripts.
