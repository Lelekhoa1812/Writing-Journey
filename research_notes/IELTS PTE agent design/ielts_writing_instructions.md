# IELTS Academic Writing — Official Rules & Coach Application

**Research scope:** IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 and Task 2 only. **As of:** November 2026.  
**Official primary sources:** [IELTS.org](https://ielts.org) band descriptors (May 2023), key assessment criteria PDF, test format pages, sample scripts with examiner commentaries, scoring-in-detail page, 2023 Writing Scales Review summary.

**Legend:** **[OFFICIAL]** = IELTS partners’ public materials. **[TEACHING]** = British Council / IDP / ielts.org educator guidance — useful for coaching, not exam rules.

---

## What are the current public band descriptors for Task Achievement, Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy?

### Takeaway

The current **public** Writing band descriptors (Bands 0–9) were **updated May 2023** after a multi-year examiner review; wording was clarified, not replaced by new criteria. Task 1 uses **Task Achievement (TA)**; Task 2 uses **Task Response (TR)**. CC, LR, and GRA share parallel scales across both tasks. A script must **fully fit the positive features** of a band; **bolded negative features in the examiner materials limit the rating** (public descriptor PDF header).

### Cited Findings

- Public descriptor PDF title page: “Updated May 2023”; same four criteria for Academic and General Training Writing — [IELTS Writing band descriptors (PDF)](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
- Operational introduction of revised descriptor wording: May 2023, following 2019–2023 review — [IELTS Writing Scales Review and Update – summary overview (Nov 2023)](https://ielts.org/cdn/Research/ielts-writing-scales-review-and-update-summary-overview-clark-et-al-2023.pdf).
- Scoring rule of thumb: “A script must fully fit the positive features of the descriptor at a particular level” — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
- Band 0 (all criteria): used only if the candidate did not attempt the task, wrote entirely in another language, or **there is proof the answer was totally memorised** — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf); also [Teacher’s guide to IELTS (PDF)](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-for-teachers/ielts-teachers-guide.pdf).

#### Official elaboration (what examiners are told each criterion *means*)

- **Academic TA:** information-transfer task tied to visual input — select key features, sufficient illustrative detail, accurate figures/trends, compare/contrast trends (not mechanical listing), appropriate format — [Key assessment criteria (PDF)](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf).
- **TR:** respond fully; extend/support main ideas; relevance; clear opening, position, conclusions; appropriate format — same PDF.
- **CC:** coherence = logical sequencing of ideas; cohesion = appropriate cohesive devices; expects paragraphing, within/cross-paragraph sequencing, reference/substitution, discourse markers — same PDF.
- **LR:** range, topic-appropriacy, precision, collocations/idiom, spelling/word-formation **density and communicative effect of errors** — same PDF.
- **GRA:** range of simple/compound/complex at sentence level, accuracy by structure type, **communicative effect** of errors, punctuation — same PDF.

### Inferences

- Coach feedback should mirror **official criterion names** (TA vs TR) and the **positive-feature threshold** (“must fully fit”) — partial compliance usually maps to the band below aspirational targets.
- At Band 7+, Academic Task 1 **overview + trend identification** and Task 2 **full prompt coverage + developed position** are the highest-leverage TA/TR differentiators in the public wording.
- LR/GRA penalties scale with **whether errors impede communication**, not raw error counts alone — consistent with examiner comment patterns (below).

### Gaps

- Full examiner **Instructions to Examiners** (non-public) are referenced in the 2023 review but not published; fine-grained rules for borderline half-band decisions between criteria are not publicly specified.
- Whether examiners award **half bands on each individual criterion** before averaging is not spelled out in public candidate materials (only that **task** scores may be whole or half bands) — [Academic sample responses PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-academic-writing/ielts-academic-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf).

## What are the official task instructions, word minimums, and recommended timing for Academic Writing Task 1 and Task 2?

### Takeaway

One **60-minute** Writing section; **both tasks compulsory**. **Task 1:** ≥**150 words**, **about 20 minutes** — describe visual information (graphs, charts, tables, process/object diagrams) in **academic/semi-formal neutral** prose, **full sentences only**. **Task 2:** ≥**250 words**, **about 40 minutes** — essay on a point of view, argument, or problem, focused on the prompt’s scope. **Task 2 counts double** toward the final Writing band; under-length answers may lack evidence for higher bands.

### Cited Findings — **[OFFICIAL]**

| Time (section) | 60 minutes total (both tasks) | same | [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing) |
| Recommended split | ~20 minutes | ~40 minutes | [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing); [Writing prep resources](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources) |
| Word minimum | 150 | 250 | [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing) |
| Task weight | 1/3 of Writing band | 2/3 of Writing band | [Writing prep resources](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources); [IELTS Australia Writing bands](https://ielts.com.au/australia/results/ielts-band-scores/writing-band-score) |
| Format constraints | No notes/bullet lists; connected text; penalties for off-topic, non-prose, **plagiarism** | same | [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing); [Key assessment criteria PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf) |

- Short answers: official text states that if the answer is **too short**, there may be **insufficient evidence** to award higher bands (not a separate “automatic penalty” table) — [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing).
- Longer Task 1 is **not penalised for length alone**, but steals time from higher-weight Task 2 — [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing).

### Cited Findings — **[TEACHING]** (not scored rules)

- Task 1: short intro (2–3 sentences) = paraphrase prompt + **overview**; body with selected data; **no conclusion required** (overview can go at end if missing upfront) — [ielts.org educator blog, Apr 2021](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/preparing-learners-for-task-1-on-the-ielts-academic-writing-test).
- Copy-pasting the Task 1 rubric does **not** count toward the 150-word requirement (educator guidance; aligns with official “copied rubric must be discounted” at low bands) — [same blog](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/preparing-learners-for-task-1-on-the-ielts-academic-writing-test).
- Task 2: use paragraphs; topic sentences; avoid overusing cohesive devices (references Band 5 CC descriptor) — [IDP IELTS Focus AC Writing T2 (PDF)](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf).

### Inferences

- A coach should time-box **40/20** and flag **Task 2 completion** as higher ROI than polishing Task 1.
- Task 1 coaching should stress **selection + overview + comparison**, not exhaustive data listing (official TA distinction between highlighting trends vs mechanical recount).

### Gaps

- Official materials do **not** publish automatic band caps for specific word-count deficits (e.g. 240 words); underlength is handled qualitatively via descriptors (notably Band 3 LR “significantly underlength”).

## Which errors cause the largest band drops, according to official or examiner-guidance sources?

### Takeaway

Official sources emphasise **task coverage failures**, **off-topic/irrelevant content**, **plagiarism/memorisation**, **non-essay format**, and language features whose errors **impede communication** — not isolated slips. Public examiner commentaries show **incomplete task response**, **weak organisation/paragraphing**, **mechanical Task 1 reporting**, **rubric copying**, and **frequent grammar errors that burden the reader** as common limiters.

### Cited Findings — **[OFFICIAL]**

**Hard penalties / floor behaviours**

- **Plagiarism** (partly or wholly): scripts **may be penalised** — [Key assessment criteria PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf).
- **Bullet points / note form**: not appropriate; penalised — same PDF; [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing).
- **Off-topic / unrelated content**: penalised — [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing).
- **Totally memorised script** (proven): **Band 0** — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
- **Copied task rubric**: must be **discounted** (Band 1 descriptor) — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
- **≤20 words**: rated Band 1 on all criteria — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).

**Descriptor-linked limiters (paraphrased “bold” themes)**

- **TR/TA:** misunderstanding prompt; **incomplete bullet/prompt coverage**; tangential answers; missing clear position; unjustified/repetitive conclusions (Task 2) — [band descriptors PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
- **CC:** **missing/illogical paragraphing**; overuse/misuse of cohesive devices; poor referencing causing repetition — same PDF; Band 5 CC explicitly ties to inadequate/overused devices.
- **LR:** **memorised/formulaic chunks**; repetitive basic vocabulary; spelling/word-formation errors that **impede meaning** — same PDF.
- **GRA:** **frequent errors causing reader difficulty**; faulty complexes; punctuation failures impeding meaning — same PDF.
- **Underlength:** Band 3 LR links inadequacy to **significantly underlength** responses — same PDF.

**Public examiner commentaries (Academic / GT, applicable criteria)**

- Task 1 Band 6: key features covered but **mechanical** reporting; overview present; repetition hurts coherence — [Academic Writing sample responses PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-academic-writing/ielts-academic-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf).
- Task 2 Band 6.5: **introduction mainly copied from rubric** (word-count adjusted); **no conclusion**; paragraphing weaknesses — examiner training commentary in [IDP AC Writing T2 Focus PDF](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf).
- GT Task 1 Band 6.5: **absence of paragraphs** “prevents the award of a higher band” for CC; **regular article/preposition errors** limit GRA — [IDP GT Writing T1 Focus PDF](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20GT%20Writing%20T1%20-%20Student%20Updated.pdf).
- GT Task 1 Band 5.5: incomplete bullet coverage; weak logic; **frequent grammar errors causing reader difficulty** — [GT sample responses PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-general-training-writing/ielts-general-training-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf).

**Research context (not a penalty schedule)**

- IELTS research on memorised material: examiners distinguish **generic memorised chunks** vs genuine proficiency; no simple automatic cap published for partial templates — [memorized learning research summary](https://ielts.org/researchers/our-research/research-reports/the-effect-of-memorized-learning-on-the-writing-scores-of-chinese-ielts-test-takers).
- Coherence & cohesion had slightly lower inter-examiner reliability than LR/GRA in a 2011 Academic Task 2 study — [Cotton & Wilson research PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Research/investigation-of-examiner-rating-of-coherence-and-cohesion-in-ielts-academic-writing-task-2-cotton-et-al-2011.pdf).

### Cited Findings — **[TEACHING / UNVERIFIED CLAIMS]**

- Third-party blogs alleging a **2025–2026 “Band 4 cap” for any template** on Task Response are **not corroborated** in public IELTS materials reviewed for this note (e.g. commercial prep sites). Treat as **unofficial** unless IELTS publishes matching guidance.

### Inferences

- Largest **single-criterion** drops in practice usually track **Task Response / Task Achievement** (unanswered prompt parts) and **Coherence** (no paragraphs / no overview), because they trigger **whole-band descriptor mismatches**.
- **GRA/LR** drops are often **gradual** unless errors **obstruct meaning** or writing is **underlength** (starving evidence).
- Coach priority order for remediation: **(1) complete the task**, **(2) organise with paragraphs + overview/position**, **(3) accuracy/range**.

### Gaps

- No official ranked list (“errors ordered by impact”); impact is **holistic per criterion** against descriptors.
- Internal weighting when one criterion is far below others (e.g. TR 5, GRA 7) is not publicly documented.

## How should an AI coach apply these rules without claiming to be an official IELTS examiner?

### Takeaway

Mirror **public criteria and language**, give **descriptor-aligned diagnostic feedback**, and present any numeric band as an **unofficial practice estimate** from an **independent** product — not an IELTS result, prediction, or examiner judgment. Disclose limitations; avoid implying partnership with British Council, IDP, or Cambridge.

### Cited Findings

- Real Writing scripts are marked by **trained, certificated IELTS examiners**; sample pack notes **many valid paths** to a band and answers are **not definitive exemplars** — [Academic sample responses PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-academic-writing/ielts-academic-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf).
- Official scoring uses **four equally weighted criteria per task**, task score = average; **Task 2 double-weighted** in final Writing band; **2–4 examiners** may mark a live script (per IDP regional guidance) — [IELTS scoring in detail](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail); [IELTS Australia Writing bands](https://ielts.com.au/australia/results/ielts-band-scores/writing-band-score).
- IELTS partners do **not** publish third-party AI scoring standards; independent prep products use disclaimers (industry pattern, not IELTS authorship): practice estimates only, non-affiliation, non-predictive — e.g. [Anglai disclaimer](https://anglai.ai/legal/disclaimer), [IELTS Mastery AI disclosure](https://www.ieltsera.com/legal/ai-disclosure).

### Recommended coach behaviours (derived from official public rubrics + disclaimer norms)

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Label output: **“practice feedback against public IELTS descriptors”** | “IELTS examiner score”, “official band”, “guaranteed/tr” score |
| Use criterion labels **TA/TR, CC, LR, GRA** and quote **public** requirements (overview, position, min words) | Imply endorsement by British Council / IDP / Cambridge |
| Explain strengths/limits using **descriptor paraphrase** (“response fits Band 6 TA because…”) | Paste full copyrighted descriptor tables |
| Flag **plagiarism, rubric copying, bullet-note answers** using official penalty language | Promise visa/admission outcomes |
| For Task 1 visuals, coach **data accuracy** and **key-feature selection** only from the prompt image | Invent statistics not in the figure |
| Show **score range or confidence** when using AI (industry best practice) | Hide that scoring is automated |
| When scoring only one task, state that the **full Writing band** also requires the other task at **1:2 weighting** | Present single-task average as full Writing TRF score |

### Inferences

- An AI coach should implement a **rubric checklist** mapped 1:1 to the [Key assessment criteria PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf) sub-bullets (overview, position, paragraphing, etc.), then attach **qualitative band estimates** per criterion.
- Feedback tone should match **examiner commentaries**: cite observable features (mechanical listing, missing conclusion, rubric repetition) tied to a criterion.

### Gaps

- No IELTS.org policy page specifically governing **AI coaching apps** was found; compliance is inferred from trademark independence and assessment process descriptions.

## Have the public descriptors or task types changed in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway

**No new Academic Writing task types or timing/word rules** were published for 2024–2026. The last **public descriptor set** change was **May 2023** (wording refresh after examiner review). **2026** changes affect **delivery mode** (optional **Writing on Paper** within computer-delivered IELTS in selected markets), **not** task content or rubrics.

### Cited Findings

- Descriptor update operational **from May 2023**; 2023 public summary describes **wording adjustments**, not new constructs — [Writing Scales Review summary (Nov 2023)](https://ielts.org/cdn/Research/ielts-writing-scales-review-and-update-summary-overview-clark-et-al-2023.pdf).
- Academic format page content (150/250, 20/40 min, task skills list) remains current on ielts.org — [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing) (accessed Nov 2026).
- Sample Academic Writing tasks PDF dated **2023** still representative (chart, graph, process diagram; agree/disadvantages essay types) — [sample tasks PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf).
- **Writing on Paper** (mid-2026 rollout, selected countries): tasks, scoring, timing unchanged; field trial reported score comparability with paper-based Writing — [IELTS Writing delivery update](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/test-types/ways-to-take-ielts/updates-to-ielts-writing-test-delivery-mode).
- Regional note: some markets phased out **standalone paper-based IELTS** in favour of computer formats while adding **Writing on Paper** as a hybrid — e.g. [IDP Canada Writing on Paper guide](https://ielts.idp.com/canada/about/ielts-on-computer-writing-on-paper) (delivery only).

### Inferences

- For product copy dated **November 2026**, cite **May 2023** descriptors as current unless ielts.org publishes a newer PDF.
- Coach content on **templates** should rely on **memorisation/plagiarism** rules in public descriptors, not unverified “2026 template penalties.”

### Gaps

- Whether new **visual complexity** (multi-chart prompts) has increased in live forms is **anecdotal** in prep blogs only — **not** documented as a syllabus change on ielts.org.
- **Pencil vs pen** for handwriting: not confirmed on ielts.org in this research pass (third-party 2025 pen claims lack primary citation here).

## Is scoring four criteria (TR, CC, LR, GR) on 0–9 rounded to the nearest half band a fair student-facing estimate?

### Takeaway

**Yes for single-task practice feedback** when the four criteria are **equally weighted and averaged** — that matches the public rule for **each Writing task**. It is **not** equivalent to an official **Writing section TRF band** unless you also combine **Task 1 and Task 2 with Task 2 weighted twice**, apply the same **rounding conventions**, and disclose **examiner vs AI** uncertainty.

### Cited Findings

- Per task: four criteria, **25% each**; task score = **average** — [IELTS scoring in detail](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail); [Key assessment criteria PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf).
- Final **Writing** band: Task 2 contributes **twice** Task 1 (official prep: Task 1 **one-third**, Task 2 **two-thirds**) — [Writing prep resources](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources).
- Implied aggregation (stated across partner sites, consistent with double weighting):  
  `Writing = (Task1_band + 2 × Task2_band) ÷ 3`, then round to nearest **0.5** — [IELTS Australia Writing bands](https://ielts.com.au/australia/results/ielts-band-scores/writing-band-score); [IELTS scoring in detail](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail) (overall IELTS rounding: **.25 → up to next half band; .75 → up to next whole band**).
- Reported task bands may be **whole or half bands** — [Academic sample responses PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-academic-writing/ielts-academic-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf).
- Live scripts: **2–4 human examiners** — [IELTS Australia Writing bands](https://ielts.com.au/australia/results/ielts-band-scores/writing-band-score).

### Inferences — app model vs official process

| App design element | Alignment | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| TR (or TA), CC, LR, GR each 0–9 | **Matches official task rubric** | Use **TA** label on Task 1 |
| Equal weighting of four criteria | **Matches official per-task rule** | — |
| Half-band rounding on displayed task score | **Plausible** for task-level reporting | Public docs don’t detail per-criterion rounding before averaging |
| Single essay scored in isolation | **Fair practice estimate for that task** | Omit Task 1 or mis-state full Writing band if only one task scored |
| No Task 2 double-weight | **Understates/overstates Writing TRF** if user treats practice Task 2 score as final Writing | Compute `(T1+2×T2)/3` when both exist |
| AI instead of certificated examiners | **Not equivalent** to test-day marking | CC especially variable; disclaim uncertainty |

### Gaps

- Exact **half-band rounding** at the **criterion** level (e.g. 6,6,6,7 → 6.25 → 6.5 task band) is widely described in prep literature but **not** explicitly worked in the public ielts.org Writing section page; treat rounding implementation as **product policy** that should follow published **.25 / .75 up** rules for consistency with [IELTS scoring in detail](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail).

## Appendix — Official scoring flow (for product engineers)

```text
Per task (T1 or T2):
  Score each of TA/TR, CC, LR, GRA (using public descriptors)
  Task_band = average(four criterion scores) → round per product policy (half bands)

Full Academic Writing (when both tasks available):
  Writing_band = (Task1_band + 2 × Task2_band) / 3 → round to nearest 0.5
  (Use ielts.org .25/.75 rounding tables for consistency with overall IELTS reporting)
```
